Arena v3 (feedback #39 #40 #42 #43). Buttons: G > "Demo: Arena v3".
1. "Pickaxe + axe" (also cobblestone), then "Start + battle now" (most of this only runs in the battle).
2. Field generator: "Tp: my generator". The block on the sea lantern is a normal block: mine it. Emerald ore / block pay
   coins like any ore (purse). Once mined, a new random block grows back after a delay (Lv1 15 s, Lv2 10 s, Lv3 7 s).
   Right-click the base for the upgrade menu (200 / 500 coins; greyed out when you can't). "Grow now" skips the wait.
   "Lucky block" toggles the proposal: 1 in 15 regrowths is a yellow glazed terracotta. Mine it: coins, an item or a trap.
3. Centre generator: "Tp: centre generator" (gold hologram on the arena's middle line). Click it to collect its pot and
   drops. "Banks +1000": it levels up at 1000 and 3000 coins in both banks together and rolls more per drop.
   "Centre gen: drop now" skips its 10 s timer.
4. Nodes: "Own all nodes" gives them to your team. West = Haste in your own field, Centre = small bank interest every
   30 s, East = your generator regrows 50% faster. "Status" shows what is held.
5. Protection: try to break the arena's floor, walls or ore piles: they come back at once, no drop, no coins.
   Build with cobblestone anywhere in the arena: that stays. The core (top of the centre pile) still regrows.
6. "Random event": it is announced with a 30 s countdown bar (raid horn) before it happens. "Time x10" for faster tests.
Feedback: regrow delays, lucky block (keep or drop), centre generator strength, node bonuses, event warning time.
