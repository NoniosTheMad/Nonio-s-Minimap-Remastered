Economy v3: ore yields, weight that eases over the match, growing bank interest. Buttons: G > "Demo: Economy".
1. Yields: "Start match", "Ore wall + pickaxes". Mine with the plain pickaxe: coal 0.1, copper 0.2, iron 0.5, gold 1, redstone 2, lapis 4, diamond 3, emerald 5; stone/deepslate 0. Fractions add up (10 coal = 1 coin). "Status" shows the pending hundredths.
2. Fortune: same ores with Fortune I / II / III = +40% / +80% / +120%. Silk Touch an ore, place it: it costs its Fortune III value (2.2x), so place + re-mine never profits.
3. Weight: "+1000 coins" (-40% speed). "Match clock +5 min" a few times: the slow eases (at 30 min, 80 coins are free and each coin weighs a quarter). Earning a lot also eases it (team average income).
4. Bank: "Go to my bank", right-click the vault. Four buttons always in the same place; greyed when there is nothing to move (empty purse / empty bank).
5. Interest: sidebar on the right (your team only): Bank, Interest %, Last payout, Next in. No chat. "Pay interest now" pays at once.
6. Interest grows: "Match clock +5 min": rate +0.5% per 5 min (1% at start, max 5%), cap per payout +50 per 5 min (50 at start). "max" next to the payout = capped.
7. Altar: "Go to my altar". The Offerings icons on the hologram now show iron/gold/diamond in their own colours.
