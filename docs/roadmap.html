Decided

Nexus
A barrier with a health bar protects the obsidian. It regenerates 20% per minute unless fully destroyed.
Scale
Typical matches run 2v2 to 5v5, and it holds up to 20 per team.
Coins
Carrying coins slows you; dying gives your purse to the killer; the bank is safe.
Coin mining
Only ore counts (building blocks earn nothing). Anti-silk-touch applied.
Coin weight
First 20 coins are weightless. After that: 0.1% slowness per coin (capped at 40%).
Coin drops
Killed by a mob or environment? A coin-bag drops for anyone to pick up. Killer gets the kill reward separately.
Bank scope
Team-wide vault (not per-player). All team members share the same bank.
Shop stays open
After buying an item, the dialog remains open (you can buy more without reopening).
Shop location
NPC shopkeeper at team base/spawn only (not in safe zones or anywhere else).
Shop: full inventory
Refuses purchases when your inventory is full. No drop or split.
Actionbar priority
When you hit the Nexus, its HP message takes the actionbar. Coin counter resumes after.
Emerald Dream
Its own dimension; you leave by sneaking for a few seconds.
Map & tone
A new map, keeping the legacy humour and lore.
Shaders
A side track. After round 2 + your feedback, new shader ideas wait until after M2 is done. M5 classes may pull specific shaders earlier.
