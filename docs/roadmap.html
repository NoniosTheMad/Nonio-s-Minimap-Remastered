Decided

Nexus
A barrier with a health bar protects the obsidian. It regenerates 20% per minute unless fully destroyed.
Scale
Typical matches run 2v2 to 5v5, and it holds up to 20 per team.
Coins
Carrying coins slows you; dying gives your purse to the killer; the bank is safe.
Emerald Dream
Its own dimension; you leave by sneaking for a few seconds.
Map & tone
A new map, keeping the legacy humour and lore.
Shaders
A side track. After round 2 + your feedback, new shader ideas wait until after M2 is done. M5 classes may pull specific shaders earlier.
