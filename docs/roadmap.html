What each milestone delivers

M2 · Playable core
Teams, match phases, the Nexus with its barrier, respawn, a win screen and a map reset. The first real match.
M3 · Economy
Coins and their weight, the bank, kill rewards and the full shop.
M4 · Progression & Arena
Field upgrades, generators, arena events, and the Emerald Dream dimension.
M5 · Classes
Basic classes picked on respawn and upgraded team-wide; one-time shop classes with their own tools and shaders.
M6 · Map & polish
The new map build, sounds, textures, balance and playtests.

Decided

Nexus
A barrier with a health bar protects the obsidian. It regenerates about 5% a minute unless it's fully destroyed.
Scale
Typical matches run 2v2 to 5v5, and it holds up to 20 per team.
Coins
Carrying coins slows you; dying gives your purse to the killer; the bank is safe.
Emerald Dream
Its own dimension; you leave by sneaking for a few seconds.
Map & tone
A new map, keeping the legacy humour and lore.
